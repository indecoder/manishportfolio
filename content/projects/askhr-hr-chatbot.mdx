## The problem

A large share of HR tickets were the same handful of requests, answered the same
way, every day. Leave balances. Payslip downloads. Policy questions with a
documented answer that nobody could find. High volume, low judgement.

## What I built

An organisation-wide HR chatbot on **IBM Watson** that resolves those requests
directly rather than routing them to a human.

The interesting work was not the conversation layer - it was the integration:

- mapping which requests were genuinely automatable versus which needed a human
- wiring the chatbot to internal HR systems so answers came from source data
- handling the **failure paths** honestly: when confidence was low or the system
  was unavailable, the chatbot escalated with context instead of guessing

<Callout type="warn" title="Confidence thresholds are a product decision">
The temptation is to make the bot answer everything. The version that survived
contact with real employees was the one that knew when to hand over.
</Callout>

## Outcome

Routine employee enquiries - leave applications and payslip retrieval among them
- are now self-service. HR operations got materially less manual overhead, and
staff got answers in seconds instead of days.

The pattern generalises, which is why this project shaped how I approach AI
integration generally: **the integrations and the escalation rules are the
product; the model is a component.**
