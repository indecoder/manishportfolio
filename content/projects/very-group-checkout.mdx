## Why checkout is different

On most surfaces a bug is an annoyance. In checkout it is lost revenue and a
support ticket, and the flow touches payment, stock, pricing and identity at
once. It is the part of a retail platform where **correctness outranks
elegance**.

## What I did

As Senior Lead Engineer I spearheaded checkout-flow delivery across **BFF and SPA
application development cycles** - designing the front-end flow and the
back-end-for-front-end layer that feeds it, rather than treating them as
separate concerns.

Working across that boundary is what makes the difference:

- the SPA gets a contract shaped for its screens, not a generic resource dump
- failure states (declined payment, expired session, out-of-stock) are designed
  in one place instead of being rediscovered per component
- changes to one layer do not require a coordinated release of both

<Callout type="info" title="The transferable lesson">
A BFF is an interface-design exercise. Everything I learned here about deciding
where a boundary belongs is what I now apply to AI integrations and platform
work.
</Callout>
