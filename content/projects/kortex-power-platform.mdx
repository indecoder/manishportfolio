## What it was

A CIO-organisation initiative to replace spreadsheet-and-email processes with
real internal applications, delivered on **Microsoft Power Platform** rather than
by commissioning bespoke software.

## Low-code is not no-architecture

The appeal of Power Platform is speed, and the failure mode is arriving at
twenty unmaintainable apps that nobody owns. Most of my contribution was making
sure that did not happen:

- agreeing a **naming and ownership convention** before the first app shipped
- modelling data once in Dataverse instead of per-app
- deciding explicitly which processes should stay manual

## Hands-on, plus mentoring

I stayed in the build - Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse - while mentoring
a group of trainee developers. The split matters: you cannot teach delivery
standards you are not personally practising.

<Callout type="tip" title="What transferred">
Teaching trainees to break a process into automatable steps is the same skill as
scoping an AI integration. Both are about knowing where human judgement is
genuinely required.
</Callout>

## Outcome

A set of internal applications in daily use across the organisation, and a
mentored team that could keep building them.
